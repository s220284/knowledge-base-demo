What was the outcome of the Northwind Industries Q1 contract negotiation, and who is the current champion on their side?
KB
Northwind Industries renewed their annual contract on March 14, 2026 at a 12% uplift over the prior term, with the addition of a multi-region data residency rider. The renewal was led by their Chief Procurement Officer, Hannah Voss, with sponsorship from EVP Operations, Daniel Marsh.

The current champion is Hannah Voss. She has driven three of the last four major decisions for Northwind, including the original 2024 onboarding and the 2025 expansion to APAC. Her engagement score in the CDP is currently 87 (top decile).

Open items from the negotiation: (1) a 30-day SLA addendum to be finalized in April, (2) a quarterly executive review cadence starting Q2.

AC
What did Hannah say specifically about the data residency rider?
KB
From the March 14 QBR transcript, Hannah Voss said: "The data residency piece is non-negotiable for the EU operating units. We need clean separation of EMEA-originated data with audit trails we can hand to our DPO. The commercial uplift is fine if the technical architecture is solid."

She followed up two days later in writing (Mar 16 email) reiterating the audit-trail requirement and asking for a technical architecture review by April 8. Daniel Marsh seconded the audit-trail concern and added that Northwind's legal team would want the rider language reviewed before signature.
